Telangana Movement and State Formation

Official TGPSC syllabus document · 84 pages · last updated 12 August 2026

1. The idea of Telangana, 1948–1970

The demand for a separate Telangana state has roots in the merger of the Hyderabad State with the Indian Union in 1948 and the subsequent formation of Andhra Pradesh in 1956. The Gentlemen's Agreement of 1956 laid out safeguards for the Telangana region in employment, education and revenue allocation.

1.1 The Gentlemen's Agreement, 1956

Signed on 20 February 1956, the agreement provided for a Regional Council, reservation of posts for Telangana candidates, and the retention of surplus Telangana revenues within the region. Its incomplete implementation became the central grievance of the movement.

Frequently asked in Paper IV

The date, signatories and the six main provisions of the Gentlemen's Agreement have appeared in four of the last five Group 2 papers.

1.2 The 1969 agitation

Beginning with a student hunger strike in Khammam in January 1969, the agitation spread across the region and led to the formation of the Telangana Praja Samithi. Over 350 people died in police action during the year.
